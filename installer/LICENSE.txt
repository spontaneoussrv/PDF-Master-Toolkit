2. YOUR DOCUMENTS AND YOUR PRIVACY

   PDF Master Toolkit processes your files locally on your own computer.
   Document content is not transmitted to SheetClub or to any third party.

   The application keeps a local history of file names, operation names and
   timestamps so that you can find your recent work. It never records the
   contents of your documents, and never stores passwords. History can be
   switched off entirely, and cleared at any time, in Settings > Privacy.


3. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES AND DOES NOT DO

   The following limits are inherent to the PDF format and are stated here so
   that expectations are accurate:

   (a) Conversion between PDF and editable formats such as Word and Excel is
       an approximation. PDF is a fixed-layout format; no converter reproduces
       every document exactly.

   (b) Compression results depend entirely on the document. A file that is
       already optimised may not get smaller. No specific reduction ratio is
       promised.

   (c) PDF permission settings (printing, copying, editing) are instructions
       to the PDF reader, not enforcement. Compliant readers honour them;
       others may not. Only an open password actually encrypts a document.

   (d) Redaction removes the underlying text and image content from the file
       rather than merely covering it. It does not remove content held in
       attachments, form fields or external references, and it cannot undo
       copies of the document made before redaction.

   (e) Metadata removal clears the document information dictionary and XMP
       packet. It does not guarantee anonymity: page content, embedded fonts
       and image data may still identify a document's origin.

   (f) Repair of damaged PDFs is attempted on a best-effort basis. Where the
       original data is missing rather than misplaced, recovery is not
       possible. The software reports how many pages were recovered and how
       many were lost.

   (g) OCR accuracy depends on scan quality, language and typeface. The
       software does not guarantee error-free text recognition.

   (h) Scanner support depends on the scanner's Windows WIA driver and is not
       available for every device.
